# Storefront-Website
A website project created for my SDC260 course 

Website Description and functionalities
This website is a book and school supply store somewhat like a college bookstore. The website has a very simplistic look and color scene.
On the home screen you will be greeted with a welcome message that gives you a description of what the site sells and how to navigate it. There are also books displayed that will be labeled under "Recent and popular releases" and those books are all equipped with appropriate pictures, prices, descriptions, and the amount in stock that updates when you add them to your cart. There are two separate sections "Books" and "School Supplies" that sell well books or school supplies. Once you are satisfied with your items added you can access your cart in the "Checkout" section which allows you to see the price of all your items as well as how they will be affected by discounts from coupons, and you also have an area to input your shipping and payment information. Lastly there is a "Contact" section for if a customer for example, needs a refund or purchased the wrong item or amount on accident.
